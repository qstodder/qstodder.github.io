FAQ

Everything you need to know for the wedding weekend.

Can I bring children?

Your children are welcome at the Welcome Gathering and Morning-After Brunch; however, our Wedding will be an adults-only celebration.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Unfortunately due to capacity constraints, we can only extend invitations to those listed on the invite.

What should I wear?

The Wedding will be semi-formal to formal. We suggest knee-length, midi, or floor-length dresses; button-up shirts with dress trousers; or lightweight and linen suits.

Suit jackets and ties are welcome but not required—please dress with the warm coastal weather in mind.

All other wedding events will be casual and beachy!
